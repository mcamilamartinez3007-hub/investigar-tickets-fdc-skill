# Client collaboration and communication

## What the delivery team provides

- initial analysis;
- confirmed findings;
- clarification request;
- validation request;
- UAT instructions;
- deployment readiness;
- root cause analysis;
- recovery results;
- known limitation;
- recommended next step;
- closure recommendation.

## What the client commonly provides

- expected functional behavior;
- authoritative-system decision;
- real cases, assets, users, routes, and dates;
- authorization or execution for client-owned objects;
- UAT and production windows;
- App/Web/Mobile/Enertia/Historian validation;
- functional acceptance, promotion approval, and closure confirmation.

## External content

Every client-facing update must state:

1. What we observed.
2. What is confirmed.
3. What it means to the business.
4. What we changed or recommend.
5. What we need from the client.
6. What happens next.

Do not send the entire exploratory path. Send a traceable and actionable conclusion.

## Rules

- Do not promise an RCA when only a probable mechanism exists.
- Do not ask the client to perform work owned by the delivery team.
- Do not assign ownership to a named person without checking validity.
- Do not hide UAT dependencies or risks.
- Do not recommend closure without naming the continuation ticket when work moves elsewhere.
